Refund Policy

Project work (websites / apps)

If work has not started after payment, you may request a full refund of that payment within 48 hours.

Once design/development has started, the advance is non-refundable because time and production costs are already used. Unused balance (if any) for unfinished milestones may be discussed case-by-case.

Instagram monthly plans

Monthly fees are for the billing month once content work has begun. Unused prepaid months may be paused once by mutual agreement; cash refunds for started months are not offered.

Ads management

Management fees for a started billing period are non-refundable. Ad spend paid to Meta/Google is never refundable through BoostByRajat — request those from the platform.

How to request

WhatsApp or email with payment reference and reason. I respond as soon as practical.
