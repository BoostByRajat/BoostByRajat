Privacy & data collection

BoostByRajat collects limited personal data to reply to enquiries and — with your consent — to send offers and run promotions.

What I collect

  • Name, email, phone/WhatsApp, city/location
  • Service interest and messages you send (forms, chat, WhatsApp)
  • Technical basics needed to run the site (standard server logs)

Why

  • Provide quotes and project support
  • With consent: offers, promotions, WhatsApp/email updates, and audience building for ads
  • Improve replies via AI assist (message text may be processed by Groq or similar provider)

Consent

Marketing/offers require an explicit checkbox on the Get offers page. Enquiry/chat without that checkbox is used for support only.

Storage

Lead records are stored for the studio’s operations. Hosting disks may be ephemeral on free plans — export backups are recommended on my side. Do not submit passwords or payment card numbers in forms.

Your choices

Email or WhatsApp to correct data or request removal from the offers list. I will stop promo messages after a clear opt-out request.
